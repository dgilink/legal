2. 처리위탁 및 제3자 제공

회사는 원활한 서비스 제공을 위해 아래 업체에 필요한 범위의 처리를 위탁합니다. 법령에 따른 경우를 제외하고 이용자의 개인정보를 제3자에게 판매하거나 제공하지 않습니다.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-13

CardScanAI is operated by DGI Link.

1. Information We Process

  • Email address and account identifiers: account identification, login, and account management; deleted upon account deletion.
  • App- or device-related identifiers: app functionality, user authentication, Google Play payment verification, service security, and fraud prevention. We do not use this information for advertising or marketing.
  • Credit balance and purchase-verification records: service management, duplicate-grant prevention, and payment verification; deleted after the purpose is met or any legally required retention period ends.
  • Business-card images and extracted information: AI card analysis requested by the user. We do not separately retain this data on our servers after processing; information saved by the user may remain in local app data and chosen device contacts.

2. Processors and Third Parties

We use Supabase for account, credit, purchase-verification, and server functions; Anthropic (Claude API) for business-card image analysis; and Google Play for consumable in-app purchase processing and purchase-status verification. DGI Link does not collect or store payment-card details directly.

3. Your Rights and Contact

You may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing, and may initiate account deletion in the app settings.

プライバシーポリシー

施行日: 2026-09-13

1. 取得・処理する情報

  • メールアドレスおよびアカウント識別情報：会員識別、ログイン、アカウント管理のために処理し、退会時に削除します。
  • アプリまたは端末に関連する識別子：アプリ機能の提供、利用者認証、Google Playでの決済検証、サービスの安全性確保および不正利用防止のために処理します。広告またはマーケティング目的では使用しません。
  • クレジット残高および購入検証記録：サービス管理、重複付与防止、決済検証のために処理し、目的達成または法令上必要な保管期間の終了後に削除します。
  • 名刺画像および抽出情報：利用者が依頼したAI名刺分析のために処理します。処理後に当社サーバーへ別途保存せず、利用者が保存した情報は端末内のローカルデータまたは選択した端末の連絡先に保存されることがあります。
